Privacy Policy

App: Aalarm - AI Auto Alarm  |  Last updated: March 2026

Aalarm - AI Auto Alarm is a personal alarm app. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

Aalarm - AI Auto Alarm stores all data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • Alarm data – alarm times, labels, and settings you create
  • Usage statistics – snooze/dismiss history, used locally to adapt snooze duration
  • Calendar data – read-only access to your calendar events, used to suggest alarms; never leaves your device

Permissions Used

  • Schedule Exact Alarms – to fire alarms at the exact time you set
  • Receive Boot Completed – to reschedule alarms after your device restarts
  • Foreground Service – to play alarm audio reliably
  • Use Full Screen Intent – to show the alarm screen over the lock screen
  • Post Notifications – to show alarm notifications
  • Read Calendar – to scan upcoming events and suggest alarms
  • Vibrate / Wake Lock – to vibrate and keep the screen on during an alarm

Third-Party Services

Aalarm - AI Auto Alarm does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Aalarm - AI Auto Alarm does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
